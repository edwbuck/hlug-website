# Advocacy

This group unabashedly promotes and advocates Linux.  That said, we are not in
the business of promoting Linux where it creates disruption without benefit.  If
you are seeking to promote Linux personally or professionally, our group can
help you promote the use of Linux in your environment.

Promoting Linux as platform comes from many motivations, but the success of any
promotional effort hinges on utility.  If the end goal does not increase the
ability to benefit the audience, any promotional effort of Linux will fail.
Items like philosophy are core to Linux's origin and history, but its utility in
solving the problems with Linux is what drives its adoption.
