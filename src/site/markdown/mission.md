# Mission

The Houston Linux User Group's mission is to promote, educate, support, and
socialize among users and businesses with a keen interest in the the Linux
operating system.  The group does not pomote particular distros, but many of
the members within the group have use multiple distros and can provide guidance
or support for a specific distro.

Whether you are keen to give a presentation, learn a technology, become a
developer, hone your administration skills, play with a home lab, create
products, network for employment, or just have fun with some Linux loving
people The Houston Linux User Group is the place to be in Houston for Linux
fun and work.

Each [meeting][meetings] is noted in our [calendar][google-calendar].  To learn
how you can add the calendar to yours, please follow our instructions to
subscribe to the calendar for your [Google account][google-add-calendar] or 
[iPhone][iphone-add-calendar] device.

## Advocacy

Promoting Linux is what we do.  As an operating system is only as useful as the 
utility it provides, this includes promotion of using the operating system and
the associated applications for specific goals.

We believe that Linux is not just an operating system for the server room, where
it already dominates as the major player in providing software infrastructure;
it is also highly usable on the desktop, where the free availablibity of most
offic suite software makes it an attractive competitor to any other platform.

## Education

For Linux to grow, people need to learn the skills and obtain the tools to use
it.  While there are a pletora of means by which one can learn Linux, sometimes
a bit of guidnance can help when approaching a specific topic, or in selecting
which topics to start with.

The group provides weekly user meetings [calendar][google-calendar] where one
can reach out to to find people who can assist or direct educational efforts.
With some help, often a educational goal can be reached faster than alone.  If
this sounds interesting to you, please consider joining use for a user meeting
or reaching us through the group's communication channels.

## Support

Even when one has skill, it is always possible to get in over one's head.  If
you are new user seeking an installation, or an old hand encountering an issue
novel issue, our group had decades of debugging and troubleshooting experience
available.  Our user meetings provide free support for any item that can be
handled within the meeting time.  

If you need more assistance, arrangements can be made for support sessions
outside of the meeting times, and depending on the details, these sessions may
be free or provide at a modest fee.  Contracts obtained through the group allow
the group to fund additional efforts, and reimburse group staff for their time.

## Socialiation

Whenever you get a number of Linux loving people together, it seems that fun
and friendship is never far away.  If you are inclined, the group has an active
social calendar with many opportunities to have fun [calendar][google-calendar].

Over the years, many of the group members have developed long lived friendships
and connections.  These extend to members that have long moved out of the
Houston area.  If you find yourselve with a bit of extra time, drop on by and
join the fun!
